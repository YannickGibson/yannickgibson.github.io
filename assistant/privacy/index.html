Personal Assistant

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy describes how this owner-operated personal assistant uses Google account data. It applies to the assistant described on the application homepage, not to unrelated portfolio projects. The assistant is for the owner’s use and does not offer public registration.

Information accessed

  • Account information: the connected account’s email address, granted permissions, and authorization metadata.
  • Gmail: message contents and metadata needed for a request, including subjects, senders, recipients, dates, labels, and relevant attachments when requested and accessible.
  • Google Calendar: accessible calendar details and events, including titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, attendees, and availability, as needed for a request.
  • Assistant records: the owner’s prompts, tool results, generated responses, and saved notes may include excerpts or summaries of this information.

Actual access is limited by the OAuth permissions granted. The initial intended use is email and calendar reading. Sending email or creating, editing, or deleting events requires an instruction from the owner and the necessary permissions.

Purpose and permitted use

Information is used to provide the owner’s requested personal assistance: finding and summarizing messages, identifying deadlines, reviewing commitments, and planning. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Personal Assistant’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Processing and sharing

The assistant runs in an owner-controlled environment. Relevant data may be sent to configured AI inference services to answer the owner’s request. The service processing a request depends on the owner’s selected provider and any enabled fallback configuration. Data processing is not limited to the local device.

Responses and relevant excerpts may also pass through the messaging service selected by the owner, including Telegram when that channel is used. Google processes API requests, and GitHub Pages hosts these public informational pages. These services may retain operational records under their applicable terms and settings.

Transfers of Google data are limited to providing the requested assistant functionality, the owner’s explicit sharing instructions, or applicable legal or security obligations. The assistant does not publish email or calendar data to this website. Before enabling a materially different use or sharing arrangement, the operator will update this notice and obtain any required consent.

Storage and protection

OAuth refresh credentials are stored in the connector’s encrypted credential store on the owner-controlled host. They are not placed in these public pages or their source repository. Account access is authorized with Google; the assistant does not ask the owner to send a Google password or authentication code in chat.

Conversation history, tool outputs, and saved notes may persist in the assistant environment and in the chosen messaging service. Relevant data is also processed by the AI services described above. Encryption of the credential store does not imply that all conversation records are encrypted or that every provider has zero retention.

Retention and deletion

OAuth credentials are retained until removed or replaced; Google may expire or revoke them earlier. Local conversation records and saved notes do not currently have a guaranteed automatic deletion interval and may remain until the owner deletes them. Messaging and AI service records follow the retention controls and terms of those services.

The owner can remove the connected account’s local credentials, delete relevant assistant conversations and notes, and use the selected services’ deletion controls. To request deletion of assistant-held data or obtain help, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen. Removing an OAuth connection does not automatically delete previously saved conversations or notes, and deleting local data does not automatically delete provider-held records.

Control and revocation

Access can be revoked through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future access using that authorization, but does not delete existing messages or events from the Google account or erase previously retained assistant records. The owner may also stop using the assistant at any time.

This public website

These two informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script, advertising, or embedded third-party resources, and set no application cookies. GitHub Pages may process standard hosting request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub privacy statement. Following an external link is subject to that service’s own practices.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the assistant’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. The operator is the account owner. For privacy questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
