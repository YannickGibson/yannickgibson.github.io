Personal Assistant

A private, owner-operated assistant for finding information in email, reviewing upcoming commitments, and personal planning.

This assistant connects to Google accounts using OAuth. It is for the owner’s own Google accounts, not a public service or an application accepting sign-ups.

This website provides app information and privacy disclosures. It does not display mailbox contents, calendar events, or account credentials.

What it does

  • Finds and summarizes relevant Gmail messages, such as coursework updates and deadlines.
  • Reads Google Calendar events and availability to explain upcoming commitments and support planning.
  • Answers the owner’s requests through a private assistant conversation.

Google account access

The owner authorizes access directly with Google. Access depends on the permissions granted in the consent screen. Email and calendar reading are the initial intended capabilities; sending messages or changing events requires the owner’s instruction and appropriate permissions. Connecting an account is not blanket permission to send messages.

The assistant uses renewable OAuth credentials so it can respond without a new browser login for every request. Authorization can be revoked at any time; uninterrupted access is not guaranteed.

How information is handled

Relevant email and calendar information may be processed by configured AI providers and included in the owner’s private conversation. It is not necessarily processed only on the owner’s computer. Read the privacy policy for details about access, processing, storage, retention, and deletion.

Owner and support

This is an independent, owner-operated personal project, not an official Google service. For privacy or support questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
